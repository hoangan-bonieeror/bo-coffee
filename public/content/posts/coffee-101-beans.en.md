> [!NOTE]
> **This post answers:** standing in front of a shelf of coffee, where do you look to pick a bag you'll enjoy? No jargon required — just five lines.

## 1. Species: Arabica or Robusta

Almost every bag is **Arabica**, **Robusta**, or a blend of the two.

- **Arabica** tends to be aromatic, gently acidic and layered.
- **Robusta** is bolder and more bitter, with a heavier body and nearly twice the caffeine.

Neither is simply "better" — they suit different ways of drinking. See [Arabica vs Robusta](#/p/arabica-vs-robusta) for more.

## 2. Origin

Country, region, sometimes the farm. The more detail, the more likely the coffee is traceable and carefully handled. A few starting points:

| Origin | Often tastes like |
| --- | --- |
| Ethiopia | Floral, citrus, berries |
| Kenya | Bright acidity, blackcurrant |
| Colombia | Balanced, caramel, red fruit |
| Brazil | Nutty, chocolate, low acidity |
| Vietnam (Robusta) | Bold, dark chocolate, pleasant bitterness |
| Vietnam (Cau Dat, Son La Arabica) | Sweet, nutty, light fruit |

These are tendencies only; variety, processing and roast can change the taste more than origin does.

## 3. Process

*Washed*, *natural*, *honey*… describe how the seed is taken out of the coffee cherry. It shapes flavor strongly: naturals are often sweet and fruity, washed coffees cleaner and clearer. More in [Washed vs natural](#/p/washed-vs-natural).

## 4. Roast level

- **Light:** keeps the bean's own character, noticeable acidity. Great for pour-over.
- **Medium:** balanced acidity and sweetness. The easiest place to start.
- **Dark:** roasty, bitter, low acidity. Suits the phin and milk drinks.

## 5. Roast date — the most important line

Coffee is fresh produce. Look for the **roast date**, not a best-before date.

> [!TIP]
> For filter coffee, many people find beans taste best from a few days to a few weeks after roasting. A bag that only shows a 1–2 year best-before was probably roasted long ago.

Freshly roasted beans still release a lot of CO₂; letting them rest a few days makes brewing more even. When you add a bag to Bo Coffee's **Bean shelf**, the app counts the days since roast for you.

## Summary

- Read in order: **species → origin → process → roast level → roast date**.
- Starting out: a medium-roast Arabica roasted within the last month.
- Buy whole beans and grind just before brewing — that's the next post.
