> [!NOTE]
> **Result:** a clean, aromatic black coffee, about 220 ml. Suits light or medium roast Arabica.

## Setup

- A V60 dripper and paper filter, a scale, a kettle (gooseneck if you have one), a timer.
- 15 g of coffee ground **medium** (like fine sand), water at 91–96 °C.

```brew-calc
method: v60
coffee: 15
```

1. Put the filter in the dripper and rinse it with hot water to remove the paper taste and warm everything up. Discard the rinse water.
2. Add the coffee, shake gently to level it, place on the scale and tare to 0.

## Steps

Press **Start** as you begin the first pour. The timer beeps and vibrates at each new step and shows how many grams to pour up to.

```brew-timer
method: v60
coffee: 15
water: 240
step: 0:00 | 45 | Bloom: spiral pour until evenly wet
step: 0:45 | 105 | Pour slowly from the centre outwards
step: 1:15 | 90 | Pour the rest, swirl gently to level the bed
step: 3:00 | | Drawdown done: remove the dripper
```

> [!TIP]
> The bloom lets CO₂ escape first, so later water reaches the grounds evenly. The fresher the beans, the more the bed puffs up.

## Dialling in

| You taste | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Sour, thin, drains faster than 2:30 | Grind finer |
| Bitter, harsh, drains slower than 3:30 | Grind coarser |
| Tasty but weak | Use 16 g of coffee, keep 240 g of water |

When you're done, tap **Save to journal** on the timer to record a rating and flavor notes — next time, **Brew again** loads this exact recipe.
